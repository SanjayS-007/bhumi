## §3 ingestion hardening + §4 bidirectional trace + §5 graph completion (2026-09-06, continued further still)

- **§3.1 — a third real document, structurally different from a Geological Report**: `NMET-DPR-SHANTIPARA-LIMESTONE-G3`, a real 214-page Detailed Proposal for G3-stage limestone exploration at Shantipara B Block, Junagadh District, Gujarat, fetched from `https://nmet.gov.in/upload/project_registration/699.pdf`. Scanned all 214 pages for restriction language (`STRICTLY RESTRICTED`, `CONFIDENTIAL`, `NOT TO BE COMMUNICATED`, etc.) — none found, classified `public`. Ingested pages 1-22 (20 real tables). **All 20 tables correctly fell through to `table_type: null, stage: unclassified` — zero candidates, zero crashes, zero force-fitting into a coal-geology table type.** This is exactly the acceptance criterion, not a near-miss: a genuinely different mineral (limestone, not coal) and document kind (an exploration funding proposal, not a geological report) produces zero fabricated structure.
- **§3.2 — partial-failure resilience, real bug fixed, not just added**: `read/pipeline.py`'s per-page loop had no failure boundary — any exception from `classify_page`/`route_page`/tier-1 extraction for one page would abort the whole document's read pipeline (nothing commits until the very end). Refactored the per-page body into `_process_page()` and wrapped its call in try/except inside the loop: a failing page is logged, queued as a `-failed` `ReviewQueueItem` (not silently dropped, not fatal), and the loop continues. Verified with a real fault injection (`tests/test_ingest_resilience.py`, monkeypatching `classify_page` to raise on page 1 of a real 2-page PDF): page 2 still ingests cleanly, page 1 is visibly flagged.
- **`--resume` was deliberately NOT built**, and this is a decision, not an oversight: `run_read_pipeline`'s transaction is atomic (nothing commits until the end), so a process-level crash mid-run leaves nothing committed to "resume" from — a fresh re-run is already the correct recovery path and is idempotent (`register_local_file`/`run_read_pipeline` delete-and-recreate).
